Track Expence

Track Expence is a personal desktop app that reads bank and payment alert emails from your own Gmail account to show your spending and deposits month by month.

What the app accesses

With your permission it uses the read-only Gmail scope (gmail.readonly) and your email address. It never sends, deletes or changes any email.

Where your data goes

Everything stays on your own computer. Emails are read directly from Google to the app on your device. Nothing is sent to any server run by the app's author, and no data is shared with or sold to anyone. Your sign-in token is stored on your computer in encrypted form.

Google API Services policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or delete the app's data folder on your computer.
